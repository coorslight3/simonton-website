Trust / Estate / Probate Sales

When an apartment building is held in a trust or an estate, the sale is about more than price. Trustees, executors and heirs are often handling a family's loss, legal requirements and a property all at once. We sell multifamily properties in these situations with care, clear communication and a plan for every step.

Trust & Estate Sales

We start with a written opinion of value built on comparable sales and rent analysis, so trustees and heirs have a defensible price range before any decision is made. We then market the building to our investor database and the major real estate websites, run a competitive bidding process, and negotiate for the highest price and best terms.

Probate Sales

Probate sales require court confirmation. We guide you through that process, attend hearings on your behalf, and keep the sale on schedule so the estate is not left waiting.

Who we help

Trustees, executors, and the heirs and beneficiaries they represent. If you are not sure where to begin, call or message us. We will explain how the sale of the building works and what to expect.

We are real estate brokers, not attorneys. Please consult your attorney or tax advisor about legal and tax questions.
