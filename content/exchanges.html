1031 Exchanges

Sell an investment property and reinvest the proceeds in another one without paying capital gains tax at the time of the sale. For owners who have held an apartment building for years, it is often what makes selling worthwhile. We plan your sale around the exchange and help you find and close on the replacement.

The clock starts the day your sale closes and does not stop. Missing a deadline generally means the sale is taxed, so start the replacement search before you go into escrow.

Why Owners Exchange

  • Keep all your equity working

    The full proceeds go into the next property, not a share to the IRS.

  • Upgrade or diversify

    Trade a management-heavy building for a newer asset, a new market or a more passive one.

  • Grow over time

    Exchange again and again so untaxed gains compound into larger properties.

Rules To Keep It Tax-Deferred

  • Never touch the cash

    A qualified intermediary holds the proceeds. Taking the money ends the exchange.

  • Reinvest it all to defer it all

    Buy equal or greater value. Cash kept or debt not replaced can be taxed.

  • Use leverage to scale

    Add a new loan on top of your proceeds to buy a larger replacement.

Exchanging Into Net Lease

A popular way to step away from day-to-day management: commercial property where the tenant pays rent plus some or all of the taxes, insurance and maintenance. The more the tenant covers, the more passive the investment, and the more its value depends on that one tenant.

Advantages

  • Predictable, long-term rent
  • Low landlord expenses
  • Often national-brand tenants
  • Hands-off ownership

Trade-offs

  • Modest rent growth
  • Value tied to one tenant
  • Lease terms are hard to change
