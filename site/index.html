The Simonton Commercial Team

The Simonton Commercial Group is a team of nine commercial real estate professionals specializing in multifamily investment sales throughout Los Angeles County.

With decades of combined experience, our team brings together extensive transaction expertise, strong industry relationships, and in-depth knowledge of the neighborhoods we serve. Each broker specializes in specific Los Angeles submarkets, giving our clients a distinct advantage when buying, selling, or evaluating investment properties.

Our approach is simple: Local expertise, strategic guidance, and results built on relationships.

Our Services

Listings & Exclusive Representation

We market your apartment building to our investor database and the major real estate websites, run a competitive bidding process, and negotiate for the highest price and best terms.

1031 Exchanges

We coordinate the timeline for exchange buyers and sellers and help you find the right replacement property. Please consult your tax advisor on your specific situation.

Multifamily Property Valuations

A written opinion of value built on comparable sales, rent analysis and a defensible price range.

Trust & Estate Sales

Experienced, sensitive handling of apartment buildings held in trusts and estates, including valuations for trustees and heirs.

Probate Sales

Guidance through court-confirmed sales, including attending hearings on your behalf and keeping the process on schedule.

Meet The Team

Chase Simonton: Vice President of Multifamily Investments

With 20 years of experience specializing in the acquisition and disposition of multifamily investment properties, Chase Simonton has established himself as one of Los Angeles's top commercial real estate agents.

Beginning his career in 2006, Chase developed a reputation for his extensive market knowledge, transactional expertise, and ability to successfully navigate complex investment sales. In 2014, he founded a team of commercial real estate professionals, further expanding his presence and leadership within the Los Angeles multifamily investment market. In 2020, he was appointed Vice President of Multifamily Investments at RE/MAX ONE.

Since 2021, Chase has consistently ranked as the company's top-producing commercial real estate agent and is recognized among the top four agents companywide at RE/MAX ONE, California's largest RE/MAX franchise by sales volume.
